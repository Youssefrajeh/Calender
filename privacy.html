Privacy Policy

Calendar Wallpaper shows your Google Calendar as a desktop wallpaper page. Last updated: 2026-09-24.

What we access

When you sign in with Google, the app asks to view and edit events on your Google Calendars. It uses this only to display your events and to add or delete events when you ask it to.

What we store

The service does not have a database and does not store your calendar data. After you sign in, a Google refresh token is placed in your personal wallpaper link and in your own browser's local storage. Calendar data passes through the server only to fulfil your request and is not logged or kept.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Your data is never used for advertising or to train models.

Your control

Anyone holding your wallpaper link can access your calendar, so keep it private. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which invalidates the link.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
